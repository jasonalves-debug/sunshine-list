Ontario Sunshine List 2025

Data current as of March 2026

Every year, the Ontario government is required by law to publish the names, positions, and salaries of public sector employees earning $100,000 or more. It's called the Sunshine List — the idea being that sunlight is the best disinfectant.

This map takes that public data and puts it where it belongs: in your hands. Every dot is a publicly-funded employer. Every name behind it is paid by Ontario taxpayers. Browse by sector, search for someone specific, or open Follow The Money to see what the numbers actually imply.

No private data was used. Everything here is already public.
